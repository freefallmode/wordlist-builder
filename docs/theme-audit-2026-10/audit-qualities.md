# Audit: Qualities

Ratings: 1 x10, 2 x48, 3 x49, 4 x21, 5 x9 (137 playable). Abstract domain, so few 1s: most property themes ("things that...") need a moment's thought, and adjective themes blur into each other.

## Redundant

- `things_with_wings` vs `things_that_fly`: same typical words (fly, plane, bird, bat, eagle). Keep `things_that_fly`; delete `things_with_wings` (or cut it to flightless winged things: penguin, ostrich, chicken, which is thin).
- `soft_materials` vs `soft_things`: cotton, silk, fur, wool, velvet in both. Keep `soft_things` (crisper for players); merge `soft_materials` into it or into `natural_materials`.
- `shiny_things` vs `sparkly_things`: diamond, glitter, crystal, gold in both; a player cannot tell them apart on screen. Merge into `shiny_things`.
- `words_for_shiny` vs `shiny_things`/`sparkly_things` adjectives: shiny, sparkling, glittering appear across them. Keep `words_for_shiny` only as the adjective theme and keep the adjectives out of the other two pools.
- `ice_words` vs `words_for_cold`: frozen, freeze, icy, frost shared. Keep `words_for_cold` (adjectives) and make `ice_words` nouns only (glacier, icicle, hail, iceberg), or merge.
- `fire_words` vs `words_for_hot`: burning shared; less severe, keep both but de-duplicate.
- `size_labels` vs `words_for_big` / `words_for_small`: small, large, big, mini in both. Keep `size_labels` limited to label words (XL, medium, jumbo, king-size, regular); drop plain adjectives from it.
- `motion_verbs` vs `ways_to_jump`: jump/hop/skip/leap belong to both; keep jump verbs out of `motion_verbs`.
- `things_that_smell_bad` duplicates `Body & Health > senses > smelly things`. Keep one (this one, it has 104 words).
- `color_terms` duplicates `Arts & Entertainment > art > color theory words` (hue, tint, shade, tone). Keep the art one; delete `color_terms` (its pool is also mixed with plain colour names).
- `patterns` overlaps `Home & Everyday > fabrics and sewing > fabric prints` (plaid, polka dot, check). Keep both only if the fabric one is cut to print names (paisley, houndstooth, gingham) and this one to everyday patterns.
- `american_candy` overlaps `Food & Drink > desserts and sweets > candy`; it is the candy theme with brand names. Merge the generic words (lollipop, jelly bean, hard candy) into `candy`, keep this only as a brand theme or delete.
- `metallic_colors` vs `precious_materials`: gold, silver, platinum shared; close siblings on purpose, but the checker will often reject them together. Fine to keep.

## Silly or unfit

- `color_terms`: catch-all (white, black, tone, hue, green); four words never point to one idea.
- `line_types`: abstract (straight, parallel, curved, dotted); reads as geometry adjectives, no group a player sees.
- `sounds`: playable catch-all (sound, noise, echo, note, tone) whose pool includes every sound sub-theme. Make it a group.
- `materials`: playable with 6 odd own words (paper, glass, rubber, ceramic, latex, enamel) and a pool of all materials. Make it a group.
- `shapes_and_patterns`: playable with own words circle, silhouette, lozenge, tangram. Make it a group.
- `sound_qualities`, `texture_words`, `motion_verbs`: very broad word classes (soft, loud, pitch; hard, rough, hairy; run, walk, fly). Too vague for a 4-word group; keep only for late levels or make groups.
- `things_that_float`: 270 words, almost anything light (cloud, leaf, log, duck, bubble); not a crisp property. Cut hard or drop.
- `things_that_fold`: paper, map, wallet, napkin; nearly any flat object folds. Weak.
- `quiet_things`: library, mouse, feather, cat; players will not see "quiet". Weak.
- `smooth_things`: ice, glass, silk, marble overlap shiny, see-through and soft; weak.
- `sweet_foods`: typical words begin "white, dark" (chocolate fragments). 819 words is a dump of every dessert; check the pool for bare adjectives.
- `taste_words`: "taste", "flavor", "delicious" plus the four tastes; works but its top words are the theme name itself.
- `blue_things`: "Sonic" is a trademark character; drop it.
- `fizzy_drinks`: top words are brands (Coke, Pepsi, Coca-Cola, Sprite); keep the theme, prefer soda, cola, lemonade, seltzer, tonic, root beer.
- `things_that_smell_bad`: fart, vomit fine for kids' humour but see Flags; sewage/sewer pair is a forms clash.
- `materials_from_africa`, `materials_from_south_america`, `materials_from_oceania`: obscure (calabash, carnauba wax, ipe, koa, jarrah, tapa cloth); no player can see the group. Drop or keep only as late-game regional colour.
- `spicy_dishes_of_africa`: harissa, peri-peri, merguez, bunny chow; expert only.
- `colors_in_turkish`: kara, sarı, mavi; unreadable without Turkish; also needs non-ASCII letters (ı, ş). Drop.

## Misplaced

- `sounds`, `materials`, `shapes_and_patterns`: should be groups, not playable (see above).
- `onomatopoeia`: playable parent with children; fine as a family parent, but its children (`eating_sounds`, `impact_sounds`) are only 2, so it cannot be a 4-member family. Move `water_sounds` and `machine_sounds` (both onomatopoeia) under it to give it 4 children.
- `voice_sounds` (laugh, cry, sing, shout) is verbs of the human voice, overlaps `Body & Health > body movement > body noises`; fits better there.
- `things_that_smell_bad` belongs with `Body & Health > senses > smelly things` (or the reverse); pick one parent.
- `fizzy_drinks` is under `tastes > food textures`; it is a drink type, better under `Food & Drink > drinks`.
- `pungent_foods` sits under `smells` but its siblings are scent lists; fine, but it could equally be under `tastes` beside `spicy_foods`.
- `spicy_dishes_of_asia`, `spicy_dishes_of_africa`, `sweet_treats_of_turkey`, `south_american_sweets`, `american_candy` are dish lists, not qualities; they belong under `Food & Drink > cuisines` / `desserts of the world` (note `sweet_treats_of_turkey` duplicates ground with `Food & Drink > cuisines > turkish food`).
- `extreme_cold_words` (blizzard, frostbite, wind chill) overlaps `Nature & Earth > weather > antarctic weather` and `snow and ice`; better under weather.
- `materials_by_region` children are trade goods (shea butter, argan oil are not materials); poor fit.
- `colors_in_other_languages` children are languages content, better under `Language & Wordplay`.

## Too thin

- `eating_sounds`: 21 words (crunch, mmm, yum, munch, gulp, burp, slurp, chomp); just enough, but "mmm", "yum" are interjections. Borderline.
- `colors_in_turkish` (26), `colors_in_french` (30, includes Bordeaux): few real colour words exist, so pools pad with forms. French is fine at about 12 good words; Turkish unfit.
- `materials_from_oceania` (32), `materials_from_south_america` (33): mostly rare or dubious (ipe, koa, jarrah, carnauba wax, coconut fiber).
- `things_that_tick` (33): watch, clock, stopwatch, metronome, kitchen timer, time bomb, longcase clock; mostly clock variants, about 8 distinct ideas. Also "tick" itself is in the pool.
- `middle_eastern_scents` (35): typical words include generic "perfume", "rose", "cinnamon"; only oud, myrrh, frankincense, attar are distinctive.
- `metallic_colors` (31): about 10 good words.
- `words_for_dark` (38): many forms (dark, darkened, darkness).

## Flags

### sensitive

- `things_that_smell_bad` (fart, vomit, sewage)
- `things_with_horns` (devil)
- `bitter_foods` (beer), `fizzy_drinks` (champagne), and check `things_that_pop` (champagne cork) and `things_that_smell_good` for alcohol
- `things_that_cut` (sword, razor; weapons)
- `fast_things` (bullet, fighter jet)
- `things_that_tick` (time bomb)
- `things_with_wings` (angel), `middle_eastern_scents` (myrrh, frankincense: religious ritual), mild
- `extreme_cold_words` (hypothermia, frostbite), mild
- `words_for_bad` (evil), mild

### englishOnly

- `onomatopoeia`, `eating_sounds`, `impact_sounds`, `water_sounds`, `machine_sounds` (sound words differ in every language)
- all `synonyms` children: `words_for_big`, `words_for_small`, `words_for_fast`, `words_for_good`, `words_for_bad`, `words_for_smart`, `words_for_strange`, `words_for_pretty`, `words_for_old`
- `words_for_hot`, `words_for_cold`, `words_for_shiny`, `words_for_dark`, `taste_words`, `texture_words`, `sound_qualities`, `color_terms`, `size_labels`, `fire_words`, `ice_words`
- `colors_in_spanish`, `colors_in_french`, `colors_in_turkish` (meaningless once the game is in that language)
- `things_with_keys` (key in the piano/keyboard sense is an English pun on lock key), `things_with_teeth` (comb, zipper, saw: English metaphor), `things_that_pop` (ear pops)
- `things_made_of_rubber` ("Wellington"), `see_through_materials` ("Saran wrap": US brand)
- `american_candy` (US brands)
